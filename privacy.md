TickMe Privacy Policy 

Effective Date: February 2026 

Information We Collect 

When you create an account, TickMe collects: 

Email address 

Authentication credentials 

A unique user identifier (UID) generated by Firebase 

This information is securely stored using Firebase Authentication (Google services). 

TickMe does not store tasks, notes, or personal content in the cloud. 
All task data remains locally on your device. 

How We Use Your Information 

We use your email and authentication data to: 

Create and manage your account 

Allow secure login 

Identify your account 

We do not sell or share your personal information. 

Data Storage & Security 

Authentication data is securely handled by Firebase Authentication. 
We do not operate our own servers. 

Permissions 

TickMe may request access to: 

Notifications (task reminders) 

Calendar (event scheduling) 

Photos (profile picture selection) 

These permissions are used only to enable app features. 

Third Parties 

TickMe uses Firebase (Google) for authentication services. 
We do not use analytics, advertising SDKs, or tracking services. 

Account Deletion 

You may delete your account at any time within the app. 
Deleting your account removes your authentication data from Firebase.
