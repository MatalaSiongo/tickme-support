# Privacy Policy for TickMe

Effective Date: [26/02/2026]

TickMe ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, and protected when you use the TickMe mobile application.

## 1. Information We Collect

### Account Information
When you create an account using Firebase Authentication, we collect:
- Your email address
- Your display name (if provided)

This information is used solely for authentication and account management.

### User Content
TickMe allows you to create and manage:
- Tasks
- Schedules
- Calendar entries
- Reminder settings

This data is stored securely and is associated only with your account.

### Notifications
If you enable reminders, TickMe requests permission to send local notifications. These notifications are generated on your device and are not used to track you.

## 2. How We Use Information

We use the collected information to:
- Authenticate users
- Save and manage your tasks and schedules
- Send reminder notifications
- Improve app functionality

We do NOT:
- Sell your data
- Share your personal data with third parties for marketing
- Use your data for advertising purposes

## 3. Data Storage and Security

TickMe uses Firebase services to securely store account-related information. Data transmission is encrypted using industry-standard security protocols.

Your task and schedule data is associated only with your account and is not publicly accessible.

## 4. Third-Party Services

TickMe uses the following third-party service:

 **Firebase Authentication (Google LLC)** – for user login and account management.

Firebase may collect certain technical information as described in Google’s Privacy Policy:
https://policies.google.com/privacy

TickMe does not control how Firebase processes data beyond authentication services.

## 5. Data Retention

Your data is retained as long as your account remains active.

If you delete your account, your associated data will be permanently removed.

## 6. Children’s Privacy

TickMe is not directed toward children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## 7. Your Rights

You may:
- Update your account information within the app
- Delete your account at any time from the app settings
- Contact us regarding data concerns

## 8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected by updating the effective date at the top of this page.
