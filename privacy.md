TickMe Privacy Policy

Effective Date: December 2025

Information We Collect

When you create an account, TickMe collects:

Name

Email address

Authentication credentials

Profile picture (if provided)

Tasks, notes, and scheduling data created within the app

This information is securely stored using Firebase (Google Cloud services).

How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

Create and manage your account

Sync your data across sessions

Enable task scheduling and reminders

Provide core app functionality

We do not sell your personal information.

Data Storage & Security

User data is securely stored using Firebase Authentication and related cloud services. Access is restricted to the authenticated user.

Permissions

TickMe may request access to:

Notifications (task reminders)

Calendar (event scheduling)

Photos (profile picture selection)

These permissions are used only to enable app features.

Third Parties

TickMe uses Firebase (Google) to provide authentication and backend services.
We do not use advertising SDKs or tracking services.

Your Rights

You may delete your account at any time within the app.
Deleting your account permanently removes your authentication data.
